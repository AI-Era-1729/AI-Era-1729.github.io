Poor note-taking slows down developer onboarding. When I was an engineer, I had to redo work because knowledge was stuck in someone's head. Now, as a leader, I see teams struggle with knowledge silos. (My own notes are still a mess.) To improve, try writing meeting notes as action items, not summaries. This week, pick one meeting and make its notes actionable.
