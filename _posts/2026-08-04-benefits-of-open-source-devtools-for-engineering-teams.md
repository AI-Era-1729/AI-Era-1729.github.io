Proprietary devtools lock you into a vendor's roadmap. When we switched to open-source tools, our team could fix bugs and add features directly. This changed our workflow because we didn't have to wait for a vendor's update cycle. (I still see teams stuck on outdated versions.) Try switching one tool to open-source this week. #OpenSource #DevTools
