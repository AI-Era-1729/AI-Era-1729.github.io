Struggling alone for 30 minutes, then asking senior devs with exact error logs in hand is a common experience for many developers. This is where the 30-minute rule for asking help comes in, a guideline that suggests developers should try to solve a problem on their own for 30 minutes before seeking help from others, a senior developer - an experienced coder who can guide, with error logs - records of what happened when the code failed. By doing so, developers can learn to identify the root cause of the issue, a root cause - the underlying reason for a problem, and provide valuable information to those they ask for help. What is the most challenging part of a problem you struggled with for 30 minutes before asking for help?

## 30 Minute Rule

Solve problems alone for 30 minutes

## Ask For Help

Then ask senior devs with error logs

## How It Works

Identify root cause of the issue

## Where It Breaks

Lack of error logs hinders help

## Why It Matters

Saves time for both parties
