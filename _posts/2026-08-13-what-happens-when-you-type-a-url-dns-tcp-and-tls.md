Imagine sending a letter across the ocean in 2 seconds. When you type a URL, a domain name system - a phonebook for the internet - helps your computer find the server, a powerful computer that stores the website, using a protocol called TCP, a set of rules for communication. Then a secure connection is established using TLS, a way to encrypt the data, so your information is protected. Finally, the website is displayed on your screen, as pixels, tiny dots that make up the images. You now understand how a website appears on your screen when you type a URL.
